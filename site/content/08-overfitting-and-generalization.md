# 8. Overfitting and Generalization

Overview of model capacity, bias-variance tradeoff, and generalization on unseen data.

## Regularization

L1 and L2 weight decay penalties on model parameters.

## Dropout

Randomly deactivating neurons during training iterations to prevent co-adaptation.

## Data Augmentation

Transforming training samples (rotations, crops, noise) to increase dataset diversity.
