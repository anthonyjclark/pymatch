Mathematical Example

Consider the function:

\[ f(x, y) = x^2 y + 4x \]

Evaluated at $x = 3.0$ and $y = -2.0$:

\[ f(3, -2) = (3.0)^2 (-2.0) + 4.0(3.0) = -18.0 + 12.0 = -6.0 \]

The partial derivatives are:

\[ \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = 2xy + 4 \implies \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(3, -2) = 2(3.0)(-2.0) + 4.0 = -8.0 \] \[ \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = x^2 \implies \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(3, -2) = (3.0)^2 = 9.0 \]
