# 7. Optimization Techniques

## Momentum

Accelerating gradient descent using momentum vectors.

### Nesterov's Accelerated Gradients

Nesterov momentum looks ahead at the future parameter position before computing gradients.

## RMSProp

Root Mean Square Propagation scales learning rates by a running average of uncentered squared gradients.

## Adam

Adaptive Moment Estimation combines momentum and RMSProp for adaptive per-parameter learning rates.

## AMSGrad

Variant of Adam ensuring non-increasing effective step sizes.
